# 1. Choose one customer segment

The roadmap's rule: don't treat these four groups as one persona. Their data, compliance, sharing and reporting needs differ, so a session with a mixed group tells you very little. Pick one and recruit only from it.

## The four segments

| | Agencies and property advisers | Property developers | Fractional property platforms | Individuals comparing assumptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The job** | Explain what an investment could look like, to a client, in a meeting | Show buyers what a unit in a project could return | Give prospects an educational return tool | Understand whether a property idea is worth pursuing |
| **What they must share** | A scenario with a client or colleague, a printout or PDF for a meeting | A branded page on their own site | An embeddable or white label calculator | Little. Maybe a link to a partner or friend |
| **Data they need** | Their own listings and local assumptions | Their own project's prices, yields and schedule | Their own verified asset data | Their own numbers, or any plausible ones |
| **Compliance sensitivity** | Medium. Anything shown to clients can be treated as advice or a promotion | High. Sales material about returns is often regulated | Very high. Return illustrations can count as financial promotion | Low |
| **What ESTA has today** | Sample data, share link, printable report, CSV, methodology | Nothing branded, no own data | Nothing embeddable, no own data | Everything it needs |
| **Strongest demand signal** | Already uses a spreadsheet or tool for this, and would pay to brand or save it | Asks to load their own project | Asks about embedding and data feeds | Comes back unprompted |
| **Easiest to recruit** | Medium | Hard | Hard | Easy |
| **What a "yes" would justify building** | Branded exports, editable property data, saved scenarios | A single project calculator | A data pipeline and legal review (a separate project) | Probably nothing paid |

## Recommendation (a hypothesis to confirm, not a finding)

**Start with agencies and property advisers.** Reasons:

1. They already do the job ESTA models: explaining scenarios to clients. So tasks can be realistic rather than hypothetical.
2. The features most likely to matter to them (share link, printable report, CSV, visible methodology) already exist, so sessions test real behaviour, not promises.
3. Their needs would reveal the B2B signal the roadmap asks about: branding, editable assumptions, saved scenarios and reports. Individuals rarely pay, and developers and platforms need your own data and legal review before anything else.
4. The compliance risk of testing a clearly labelled educational tool with them is low.

**What would change this recommendation:** you can't reach 5 advisers within a couple of weeks (then use individuals, and be honest that willingness to pay is weak evidence for them), or you already have a warm relationship with a developer or platform who will give you real time (then test that segment, and expect the answer "we need our own data", which is itself a result).

Whichever you pick, write it down before the first session. If you change segments halfway, start the count again.

## Screener (agencies and advisers)

Ask before booking. Accept only people who answer yes to the first three.
1. Do you explain property investment numbers to clients or colleagues at least once a month?
2. Do you use a spreadsheet, calculator or software to do it today?
3. Can you spend 45 minutes on a video call and share your screen?
4. Have you seen ESTA before? (If yes, don't include them.)

Note how they were recruited (`network`, `community`, `cold`, `friend_family`). Friends and family are kind. Try for people who owe you nothing, and the summary will warn you if more than half are friends.

## Outreach message

> Hi [name], I'm building a free learning tool that shows how a property investment could play out over ten years under assumptions you choose. It uses invented sample properties, nothing is for sale, and it isn't advice. I'd value 45 minutes of your time to watch you use it and hear what is unclear or missing. I'm testing the tool, not you, and I'd ask your permission before recording anything. Would you be open to it?

Don't promise features, and don't describe it as an investment product.

## If you test individuals instead

Swap tasks 1 to 7 in `02-session-guide.md` for the versions marked "individual", skip the willingness to pay questions about branding (ask instead what they would pay for, and what they pay for today), and expect fewer sharing needs.
