# 5. Consent and privacy

This is practical guidance for running sessions, not legal advice. Check what applies where you and your participants are. Recording consent and data protection rules vary.

## Read this aloud before recording
> "I'd like to record the screen and our voices so I can write up accurate notes afterwards. Only I will see the recording. I'll delete it within [30] days, and I'll store the notes under a participant number, not your name. You can ask me to stop recording or to delete anything at any point, and you can stop the session whenever you like. Is it OK to record?"

Get a clear yes on the recording itself, not just a yes to taking part. If they say no, take notes by hand and continue.

## Keep it minimal
- Collect no more than you need: a participant ID, their segment, how they were recruited and the date.
- Keep names, emails and any consent records in a separate file from `sessions.csv`. The summary script only needs the ID.
- Don't ask them to enter real client details or real property addresses into ESTA. Use the sample properties.
- Don't record anything on their screen that isn't ESTA. Ask them to close other windows.
- ESTA itself has no analytics and sends nothing from the simulator to a server. Scenario links carry assumptions in the address, so ask them not to put anything personal into a link they share.

## Storing and deleting
- Keep recordings somewhere private. Delete them on the date you promised.
- Delete notes for anyone who asks.
- Don't publish quotes that could identify someone without their say so.

## Being straight with participants
- Tell them the properties are invented and nothing is for sale.
- Don't present the tool as advice, an offer or a prediction.
- If you pay or reward them, say so up front and don't tie it to the answers they give.
