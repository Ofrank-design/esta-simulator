# 2. Session guide (45 minutes)

One facilitator, one note taker if you can get one. Same script for everyone, so the results can be compared.

| Time | Part |
| --- | --- |
| 5 min | Welcome and consent |
| 5 min | Their current workflow |
| 20 min | Tasks |
| 5 min | Comprehension check |
| 10 min | Debrief and willingness to pay |

## The rules that make this valid
- **Don't guide.** Read the task, then stay quiet. If they ask "how do I...?", answer with "What would you try?"
- **Don't explain the tool.** If a label confuses them, that is the finding. Write down what they said it meant.
- **Count help.** If they are stuck for about 90 seconds, ask "What are you looking for?" and log one help request. If they are still stuck, show them and mark the task `partial` or `failed`.
- **Think aloud.** Ask them to say what they are thinking and what they expect to happen. Prompt with "What are you thinking?" when they go silent.
- **Don't sell.** Never describe a feature as coming, and never quote a price. Ask about their past behaviour instead.
- **Record what they say, not what you think they meant.** Write verbatim quotes.

## 1. Welcome and consent (5 min)
Read `05-consent-and-privacy.md` aloud and get a clear yes before recording. Then:

> "Thanks for doing this. We're testing the tool, not you, so you can't get anything wrong. Everything in it uses invented sample properties and none of it is real or for sale. Please think aloud as you go. I'll ask you to try a few things, and I may stay quiet while you work. That's on purpose."

## 2. Current workflow (5 min)
- "Tell me about the last time you explained an investment scenario to someone. What did you use?"
- "What did you show them? What did they ask that you couldn't answer quickly?"
- "What did you send afterwards, if anything?"

(Listen for: tools they use today, what they hand over, who else sees it. These tell you what ESTA has to replace.)

## 3. Tasks (about 20 min)
Open the start link for each task from `scenario-links.md`. Start a timer when you finish reading the task, stop it when they say they're done or you stop them. Record `done`, `partial`, `failed` or `skipped`, the seconds, and the help requests.

| # | Agency or adviser version | Success looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T1** | "A client has about $5,000 to put in. Pick a property you'd show them and set it up." | They open a property's simulator and set the starting investment near $5,000 |
| **T2** | "The client thinks the property will be rented less than the sample assumes, about 75% of the time. Show them what that does to the first year income." | Occupancy is 75%, and they can say income went down (about 19%) |
| **T3** | "Show the client a cautious and an optimistic version next to your numbers." | They reach the compare page and their assumptions are still there |
| **T4** | "Send this exact scenario to a colleague." | They copy the link, and it opens (in a private window) with the same assumptions |
| **T5** | "Give the client something to take away." | They produce a report or a CSV, or say clearly what they would need instead |
| **T6** | "The client asks, 'where does that net yield number come from?' Find an answer." | They reach the methodology or a "Learn more" explanation, and can read it back |
| **T7** (if time) | "What if the income were reinvested instead of paid out? What changes?" | They switch the setting and can say ownership grows and no cash is paid |

**Individual version:** T1 "You have about $5,000 and want to see if this property idea makes sense." T2 "You think the rent will be vacant more often than the sample says." T3 "See a cautious and an optimistic version." T4 "Send this to a partner so they see the same thing." T5 "Keep something to look at later." T6 "You want to know where that net yield figure comes from." T7 unchanged.

While they work, note: where they hesitate for more than 10 seconds, what they click that does nothing, what they say out loud, and any term they misread.

## 4. Comprehension check (5 min)
Ask the six questions in `03-comprehension-check.md` while the simulator is still open. Don't help. Score each 0, 0.5 or 1 using the answer key.

## 5. Debrief (10 min)
Trust and clarity
- "Was there a number you didn't believe, or didn't know where it came from?"
- "What would you have wanted explained that wasn't?"

Sharing
- "Who would you show this to? What would you actually send them?" (Tag what they name: link, pdf, csv, screenshot, slides, email.)

Use again
- "Would you use this again? For what? What would stop you?" (Record yes, maybe or no, and the blockers.)

Willingness to pay, using past behaviour and not hypotheticals
- "What do you pay for today to do this job, or what do you build yourself?"
- "If this carried your own branding, who would care? Have you paid for something like that?" 
- "Do you need to change the assumptions to match your own listings? How do you do that today?"
- "Would you need to save scenarios and come back to them, or share them with a team? What happens today when you need that?"
- "If a version existed that did that, who would sign off on paying for it?"

Rate each of `branding`, `editable_assumptions`, `saved_scenarios`, `downloadable_reports` as `no`, `maybe`, `yes`, `already_pays`, or `not_asked`. Use `yes` only for a clear, specific need. Rate the four gate needs (cross device, team sharing, controlled property data, identity) as `none`, `nice` or `must`. A `must` means something they can't do their job without, and they said so unprompted or gave a real example.

Close: thank them, ask if you may contact them again, and stop recording.

## After each session (within an hour)
Fill in one row of `sessions.csv` while it's fresh. Don't wait until all the sessions are done. Add the three quotes that best show what happened.
